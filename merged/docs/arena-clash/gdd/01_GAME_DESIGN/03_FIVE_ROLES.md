# Five differentiated noncombat roles

| Role | Silhouette | Ability | Impact | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Runner | bright golden scarf + oval shoes | Dash Trail | brief speed burst leaving visible allied safe path | 18s cooldown; cannot speed banner carry |
| Sentinel | turquoise shield crest | Shelter Flag | creates 3-second friendly objective protection from interruption | 26s cooldown, cannot cover more than one zone |
| Builder | orange bridge pack | Quick Bridge | creates a short 7-second bridge across marked map shortcuts | 24s cooldown; all teams can visually see it |
| Trickster | plum prism hat | Decoy Beacon | fake objective pulse on opponent HUD for 2 seconds | 30s cooldown, never shows fake quiz information |
| Navigator | sky-blue compass cap | Route Scan | reveals next zone rotation for team 5 seconds early | 24s cooldown, not a hidden-rank boost |

All roles have baseline equal movement and objective capture. Each has readable icon, no faces, keyboard/touch activation and cooldown ring with numeric text. Role choice in lobby only; teacher may allow a single neutral reselect before match begins. Avoid one role being mandatory: solo role simulations must exceed minimum completion performance at every map. Balance on contribution and experience, not raw damage.
