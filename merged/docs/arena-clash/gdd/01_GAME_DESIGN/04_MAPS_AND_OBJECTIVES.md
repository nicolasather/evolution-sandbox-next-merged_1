# Original map book

### Meadow Mosaic
Spring-green orchard maze with cream walkways and storybook bridges, friendly clear terrain; central fountain hub and three flower-flag objectives. Props: windmills, leaf clusters, arch bridges, woven banners. Symmetric team routes.

### Clockwork Carnival
Bright clockwork plaza with moving turntables and golden gear gates, readable station labels; objective beacons are rotating colored stage lights. Motion decoration must not confuse hitboxes.

### Crystal Lagoon
A lush turquoise canal village with lilac glass isles, small floating drawbridges, light bridges, colored crystal clusters and team vaults. Water is non-damaging and clearly solid/non-solid.

Each map needs (a) screenshot-worthy full-scene art, (b) nav graph/obstacle collision, (c) normalized mirrored spawn points, (d) 3 objective placements, (e) designated safe onramps, (f) reduced-motion alternative, (g) 375px map-view camera. Avoid art detail hiding player collision. 2D parallax only outside gameplay area.
