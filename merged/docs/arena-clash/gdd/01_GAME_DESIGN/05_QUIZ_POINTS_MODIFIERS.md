# Curriculum quiz-first mechanics and points economy

Source: published user-created quiz, real snapshot, five formats supported. Synchronized question breaks atomically freeze world. Correct answers produce server-computed fraction 0..1 and normalized team wallet; partial credit only if creator enabled. No speed bonus. Every question remains 50–75% of total score. No fake “right” based on client input. Explanation after reveal, not during response.

Spend points (the ONLY currency) on four nonviolent tactical helpers: short trail speed, area capture marker, route reveal, deposit guidance. A team may buy at most one advantage per planning phase; boosts expire after one episode, max 15% impact, never confer answer advantages. Teacher selects modifiers AFTER format selection: allow a restricted subset only when compatible with synchronized rounds. Disallow answer-slot lockouts in compulsory whole-class questions unless teacher explicitly enables and all players have equal access; default OFF. All existing modifiers require compatibility mapping and deterministic replay if supported.

Prevent quiz exploitation: one committed submission per player/question/window, fixed answer deadline, server-only grading, ensure no answer keys in viewers or logs. Review cycle must disclose repeats to students and avoid score inflation by normalizing repeated answers.
