# Match rules and anti-snowball

Teams of 1–5, 2–4 teams, max 20. Co-op is NOT an Arena Clash format—do not silently add it. Teams always have symmetrical starting positions, objectives and travel distances as computed from mirror transforms. Hidden direction/route advantage is prohibited. A role can be duplicated, all roles can complete a solo objective. Team score = mean eligible player quiz accuracy × available quiz points, not raw submissions; idle/inactive players do not earn extra. Arena contribution scales by map events not roster size; occupying a zone with 5 does not fill a meter 5x faster.

Tie breaker: quiz accuracy, then objective pulses, then an explicitly displayed tie result. A time-limited match may end in a tie; no random winner selection.

A trailing team's choices remain interesting: staggered checkpoints and 4-second neutral reset windows offer tactics; no hidden rubber-banding. Students are not eliminated or excluded from answering for physical skill. Provide a low-pressure preset that removes contention on simultaneous contact without turning into a different mode.

Teacher can pick 15, 20, 30, 45 minutes; pilot may also offer 5 and 10 minutes. Pacing planner computes question count from kind mix and leaves time for reading. Never invent quiz questions. If too short offer teacher a clearly consented review cycle or shorter match.
