# Production sprite sheet and motion plan

## Five role rigs
Each rig: idle 4 poses, move 6 poses × cardinal rotation, cast 4, success 3, slow/freeze 2, reconnect 2. Equipment and silhouette distinguish role without facial features. Original cape/shield/pack/compass/scarf designs.

## Map props
At least 15 props per map, 3 zone glyph variants, 3 banners, 4 capture ring states, 3 deposit machines, 4 path tiles, 3 bridge stages, 5 ability VFX, 4 upgrade signs. Assets must render with appropriate z-order and collision hit areas separate from drawing geometry.

## Interaction affordances
Action radius appears on entering an objective, capture shows ring and numeric percentage, 2s zone rotation warning, visible own-team and rival ownership indicators, accessible labels. No fake or deceptive point totals. Mobile joystick must be attached to real movement events and pointer capture; reduced-motion choice persisted.

## Testing visual polish
At 50% screen size, distinguish all 5 roles and 3 objective states. Screenshot comparisons should check identical art language across host, projector, quiz and results, not merely gameplay. Require actual original asset license manifest, source SVG files, and a contact sheet.
